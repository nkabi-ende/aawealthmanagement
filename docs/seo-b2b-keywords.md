# AA Wealth — B2B organic search / recruitment SEO map (November 2026)

## Search goals

The website has two distinct search intents:
1. **Job-seeking / recruitment**: people in Durban and KwaZulu-Natal searching for funeral or life insurance field sales agent opportunities.
2. **Commercial B2B / broker service procurement**: insurance brokers in South Africa seeking website development, digital marketing, CRM implementation, lead generation, assignment, retention and sales enablement.

Do not promise Google rankings or describe keywords as having verified search volumes; this map is based on natural search intent and competitor-language review, not paid search-volume data. Track actual queries and clicks in Google Search Console after indexing.

## Route: /for-agents

Primary topical focus:
- funeral insurance sales agent jobs Durban
- life insurance sales representative jobs Durban
- field insurance sales agent opportunities KZN

Helpful supporting phrases (work naturally into useful text, not a keyword string):
- commission only insurance sales agent
- funeral cover sales jobs Durban
- direct sales representative insurance KwaZulu-Natal
- field sales representative Newlands West / Durban (only if genuine hiring coverage exists)
- insurance sales careers Durban
- entry-level insurance sales opportunity (only when inexperienced applicants truly are considered)

SEO title:
"Funeral & Life Insurance Sales Agent Jobs Durban | AA Wealth"

Meta description:
"Explore commission-only funeral and life insurance sales agent jobs and field sales representative opportunities in Durban, KZN with AA Wealth. No guaranteed salary."

Page checks:
- One descriptive H1 with funeral, life and sales-agent terms.
- H2 with Durban/KZN and representative opportunities.
- Role responsibilities, suitability, commission-only statement, location and enquiry steps all visible in HTML.
- Valid canonical and sitemap listing.
- Structured data uses WebPage/Organization; **do not add JobPosting markup** for a generic opportunity page. A future single verified vacancy can have its own page and JobPosting schema with the exact role, dates, location, remuneration type and hiring organisation, subject to Google eligibility rules.
- Do not mark this commission-only opportunity as salaried, permanent employment or training guaranteed.

## Route: /for-brokers

Primary topical focus:
- CRM for insurance brokers South Africa
- insurance broker lead generation
- insurance brokerage website development

Secondary search clusters:
- CRM for life insurance brokers
- CRM for funeral insurance brokers
- insurance lead management software / lead routing setup
- Zoho CRM setup for insurance brokers
- funeral insurance lead generation South Africa
- life insurance leads for brokers South Africa
- insurance broker digital marketing
- social media marketing for insurance brokers
- insurance sales agent onboarding and training materials
- insurance broker website SEO
- insurance broker sales dashboard and marketing automation
- broker CRM integration with quote forms / WhatsApp

SEO title:
"Insurance Broker CRM, Leads & Websites | AA Wealth"

Meta description:
"CRM for life and funeral insurance brokers in South Africa. Insurance website design, lead generation, CRM setup, lead management, social marketing and agent training."

Page checks:
- H1 describes websites, CRM and lead generation for insurance brokers.
- Visible H2/H3 service copy maps naturally to the CRM, lead-generation and website intent clusters.
- Important services have an actionable related CTA (scoped enquiry); no false "unlimited insurance leads", "ready-made guaranteed leads", or conversion/volume promises.
- Canonical metadata and Service/WebPage JSON-LD give Google an unambiguous page topic without fake Offer prices or reviews.
- Subsections discuss organic search, campaigns and lead routing transparently.
- Clearly describe agent sales-process materials as distinct from accredited FAIS or CPD training.
- Internal links from navbar/footer and one-broker-page service anchors are in genuine <a href> links.

## More pages worth considering after Search Console data

If specific query clusters demonstrate demand and the services are operational, build focused, unique pages rather than duplicating the generic broker page:
- /broker-crm-services — comparisons and implementation workflow, Zoho + alternative CRMs, real integration constraints.
- /insurance-lead-generation — campaign setup, lead-quality and assignment process, privacy and attribution.
- /insurance-broker-websites — mobile design, SEO, enquiry UX, analytics, before/after evidence.
- /agent-sales-enablement — field and digital playbook, operations coaching, performance reporting.

Only publish new pages when they add substantive distinct customer value; avoid doorway pages with almost identical copy.

## Post-merge Google verification checklist

1. Confirm production HTTP 200 and HTML prerendering on both routes, including title/description/canonical/h1.
2. Confirm live sitemap: https://aawealthmanagement.co.za/sitemap.xml includes both URLs and robots.txt does not exclude them.
3. In Google Search Console, validate ownership, submit the sitemap and inspect each URL. Request indexing if appropriate.
4. Inspect structured data using Google's Rich Results Test / Schema Markup Validator. Schema is not a ranking guarantee.
5. Verify mobile presentation, accessible navigation, Core Web Vitals and clickthrough to WhatsApp or mail.
6. Track real queries, impressions, clicks and landing-page conversions in Search Console/GA4 after Google processes updates.
7. Revisit the page copy using real search-query evidence every 4–6 weeks initially. Avoid keyword stuffing and misleading vacancy markup.
